Gardening and landscaping services in Dubai

Sunny Star Landscaping LLC helps homeowners and property managers care for outdoor spaces across Dubai. From garden maintenance and planting to irrigation, lawn care and landscape improvements, we focus on clear communication and practical finishing.

What we offer

Our gardening services in Dubai include landscape design and installation, garden and villa maintenance, lawn care, irrigation systems, tree and planting work, artificial grass, gravel design, and garden renovations.

  • Landscape Design & Installation: Garden layouts, planting and landscape improvements shaped around your outdoor space.
  • Garden & Villa Maintenance: Routine care to keep gardens tidy, healthy and welcoming.
  • Lawn Care & Grass Cutting: Lawn upkeep and grass-area refreshes for a clean, cared-for appearance.
  • Irrigation Systems: Irrigation installation and related support to help care for planted areas.
  • Planter Beds & Decorative Finishes: Planter-bed renovation with stone detailing and decorative wood mulch.
  • Tree & Planting Services: Tree replacement and new planting to refresh outdoor areas.
  • Renovation & Makeover: Refresh existing landscape areas with practical improvements and finishing touches.
  • Indoor Planting: Bring natural greenery into interior spaces with considered plant placement.

Areas we serve

We provide landscaping and garden care across Dubai communities, including Palm Jumeirah, Emirates Hills, Dubai Hills Estate, Arabian Ranches, The Meadows, The Springs and nearby neighbourhoods.
